**Gadolinium-based contrast agents** are injected for MRI scans and pass largely unchanged through the body and through conventional wastewater treatment, so they are increasingly detected in rivers and drinking-water sources. During a research attachment at the **Laboratoire Réactions et Génie des Procédés (LRGP)**, Université de Lorraine, in Nancy, France, I studied **adsorption using activated carbon derived from biomass** to remove these contrast agents from wastewater.

The attachment ran from November 2023 to February 2024, mentored by Prof. Marie-Noëlle Pons, with a scholarship from the Embassy of France to Malaysia.
