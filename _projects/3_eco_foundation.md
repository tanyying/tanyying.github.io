Foundation models are reshaping how science is done, but a model that is good on benchmarks is not automatically useful for ecology and sustainability. As part of a large interdisciplinary team, I co-authored a perspective on what it would take for foundation models to be **ecologically meaningful**.

> Gardiner, R. J., Cerbone, H., Akande, H. A., …, **Tan, Y. Y.**, …, Parkinson, R. H. **Towards ecologically meaningful foundation models.** _Nature Machine Intelligence_ (2026, accepted).

The project connects to a thread that runs through all my work: AI is most valuable in science when it is grounded in **domain knowledge**. The same idea drives knowledge-guided machine learning, and it was the theme of the 2025 KGML Workshop, where I won 1st place in the hackathon.
