During a research internship at **Polytechnique Montréal**, Canada, from February to June 2022, mentored by Prof. Gregory S. Patience, I worked on two projects:

- **Novel catalyst design and their activity in Fischer–Tropsch synthesis.** Fischer–Tropsch synthesis converts syngas (CO and H₂) into liquid hydrocarbons and fuels, and its yield and product distribution depend strongly on the catalyst.
- **Green adsorbent from lactose waste for wastewater treatment.** Turning a dairy-industry by-product into an adsorbent for cleaning wastewater.

The internship was supported by a scholarship from Polytechnique Montréal.
