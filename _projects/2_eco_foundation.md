Foundation models are reshaping how science is done, but a model that is good on benchmarks is not automatically useful for ecology and sustainability. In September 2025, I attended the **Shaping the Future of AI for Conservation** workshop at the **University of Oxford**. The workshop brought together a large interdisciplinary team, and from it we co-authored a perspective on what it would take for foundation models to be **ecologically meaningful**.

> Gardiner, R. J., Cerbone, H., Akande, H. A., …, **Tan, Y. Y.**, …, Parkinson, R. H. **Towards ecologically meaningful foundation models.** _Nature Machine Intelligence_ (2026). [doi:10.1038/s42256-026-01317-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01317-6)
